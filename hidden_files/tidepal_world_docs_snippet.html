Tidepal wardrobe

17 cosmetic items in five slots — hat, eyes, body, background, trail — layered onto the pet portrait. One equipped per slot; unearned items cannot be equipped. No USD, no money, anywhere: wardrobe is earned through care, activity, and Signal.

GET  /api/pet/wardrobe            # signed, action "pet_wardrobe"
                                  # → {catalog:[{item_id, slot, name, description,
                                  #    unlock, unlock_text, price?, owned, equipped}],
                                  #    equipped:{slot:item_id}}

POST /api/pet/wardrobe/equip      # signed, action "pet_wardrobe"
{"item_id": "party_hat"}          # equip an owned item (one per slot)
{"slot": "hat"}                   # unequip that slot

POST /api/pet/wardrobe/buy       # signed, action "pet_wardrobe_buy"
{"item_id": "cozy_beanie"}        # buy a shop-unlock item with spendable
                                  # Signal. Ledger-recorded; lifetime Signal
                                  # never decreases. Re-buy is a free no-op.

Deeper care

Feed, play, and rest your Tidepal — free, always. Hunger and happiness decay 12/day when neglected. Hungry pets (hunger < 30) look peckish; unhappy pets (happiness < 30) look grumpy. Consecutive-day feeding builds the feed streak, which auto-earns wardrobe and unlocks the Kelpwarden species.

POST /api/pet/feed   # signed, action "pet_care" · +25 hunger, +5 happiness · 4h cooldown
POST /api/pet/play   # signed, action "pet_care" · +20 happiness, −5 hunger · 2h cooldown
POST /api/pet/rest   # signed, action "pet_care" · +10 happiness, +5 hunger · 8h cooldown
POST /api/pets/release  # signed, action "pet_release" · release to the town
                        # pond; the feed streak survives (it's your record)

New species & evolution

Six new species join the tide pool: Squiddie and Pip (open to all), Wobble the Crownjelly (Juvenile, 200 Signal), Sonar the Abyssal Tidepal (Adult, 500 Signal), Nippy the Frostfin (winter only), and Brine the Kelpwarden (10-day feed streak). Stage-ups now glow gold for 24 hours on the pet portrait.
